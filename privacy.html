Privacy Policy
Last updated: September 2026

What We Collect

When you request a quote, contact us, or use our client portal, we collect the information you provide directly: your name, email address, phone number, moving addresses, move date, and details about your inventory or move. If you complete a move with us, we also retain photos, digital signatures, and tracking records related to your shipment for liability and claims purposes.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to quote requests and provide you with pricing and scheduling information.
  • To acknowledge receipt of your inquiry and keep you updated on the status of your move.
  • To send you a request for a review after your move is complete.
  • To document chain of custody, proof of pickup, and proof of delivery for your shipment.
  • To process and resolve claims filed through our client portal.

Email & Text Message Communications

If you provide your email address or phone number through our website's quote or contact forms, we may send you transactional messages related to your move, including request acknowledgments, status updates, and a post-move review request. We do not send marketing or promotional messages. Message and data rates may apply for text messages. You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP, and you can unsubscribe from email at any time using the link in any message we send.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the crews, referral partners, and service providers directly involved in planning and executing your move, and with our messaging and scheduling software providers solely to deliver the communications described above.

Data Retention & Security

We retain move-related records, including photos and signatures, for as long as reasonably necessary for liability, insurance, and claims purposes. Your information is stored in access-controlled systems, and photo/document storage is private by default, accessible only to authorized staff or through a time-limited secure link.

Your Choices

You may ask us to correct or delete your information at any time by contacting us using the details below, subject to our need to retain records related to a completed move for legal or insurance purposes.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your information can be sent to us through our contact form.
