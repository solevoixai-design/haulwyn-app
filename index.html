Fine Art, Diamonds & Museum-Grade Transport

The name is the promise: provenance, documented.

In the art and jewelry world, "provenance" means a verified, unbroken record of custody. That's exactly what we build for every high-value item we move — whether it's crossing town or crossing the country, insured, photographed, and signed for at every hand-off.

Fine Art & Antiques

Custom crating, climate-aware handling, and white-glove installation for paintings, sculpture, and antique furniture — including pieces too fragile or valuable for a standard freight carrier.

Diamonds & Fine Jewelry

Discreet, secure transport for individual pieces or full collections, with a signed chain of custody at every transfer — the same standard of accountability used for high-value asset transit.

Museum & Institutional Loans

Point-to-point handling for gallery and museum loans, private collection transfers, and estate-to-institution moves, coordinated directly with curatorial and registrar staff.

We built our entire moving process — binding written quotes, photographed inventory at both ends, signed chain of custody, and a coded photo record for every packed box — around one idea: your possessions deserve a documented history, not just a delivery. That's the same standard whether we're moving a bedroom set or a $1 million piece bound for a gallery wall.

Frequently Asked

Straight answers, before you call.

Does Blue Provenance Transport move fine art, jewelry, and antiques, or just household goods?

Both. Alongside standard residential and estate moves, we offer dedicated, insured, museum-grade handling for fine art, diamonds and fine jewelry, and antiques — including gallery and museum loan transport with a documented chain of custody at every hand-off.

Is the quote actually binding, or can the price change on move day?

Your rate is locked in writing before move day. Any change requires a signed change order you approve on the spot — there is no "the crew found extra inventory" surcharge.

What areas does Blue Provenance Transport serve?

All five NYC boroughs and Northern New Jersey today, with long-distance and nationwide moves available, and active expansion underway to the Hamptons, Greenwich CT, Palm Beach, and California.

Are you licensed, bonded, and insured?

Yes, and full replacement value protection is available on every move, backed by our photographed pickup-to-delivery record. Licensing and insurance credentials are available on request.
