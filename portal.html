Cargo Checkpoints

A photo or short video of the truck's actual cargo area at the two moments that matter most — fully loaded and about to leave, and arrived at your new place before anything comes off. A quick walkthrough video is real footage of your things in the truck, even though it isn't a live feed.

Photographed Inventory

Every item is timestamped and, wherever practical, photographed at pickup and again at delivery — a chain-of-custody record tied to its own QR code, so if a question ever comes up about an item's condition, there's a real record to point to rather than two people's memory of it.

Pack & Label Your Boxes

Print a sheet of codes now, days before your move, and stick one on each box as you pack it — no app to download, nothing to scan yourself. Tell us which room each box is headed to at the new place, and the crew will see that the instant they scan it off the truck, so nobody has to guess or ask.
