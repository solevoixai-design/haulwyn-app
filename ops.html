GPS clock-in/out logged from the Crew Field App, at the crew-team + job level (crews are teams, e.g. "Crew Alpha" — there's no individual crew-member login yet). An open row with no Clock Out means that crew is still on the clock. The GPS Check column compares the coordinates captured at clock-in/clock-out against the job's own geocoded origin/destination address — a spot-check for buddy punching, a wrong job selected, or a GPS/location-permission problem, not proof of wrongdoing on its own; a phone with location services off, a large building, or a legitimately nearby parking spot can all trigger it too. The Meal Break column shows whether a break was logged for that shift via the crew app's Start/End Break buttons — a shift over 6 hours with no break logged is flagged, since some jurisdictions (including New York) require an uninterrupted meal period on a shift that long; this is informational, not a substitute for checking the exact rule that applies to your state and contract. The Spread of Hours column flags a shift whose total span (clock-in to clock-out, including any break time in between) exceeds 10 hours — New York's Minimum Wage Order requires one additional hour of pay at the basic minimum wage rate for a workday with a spread that long, distinct from overtime (which is about total hours worked, not how the day is spread) and distinct from the meal-break rule above; this is informational, not a substitute for checking the exact rule that applies to your state and contract.

Export to QuickBooks

to
Every mover-specific platform researched treats "QuickBooks sync" as table stakes handled by buying, not building — so rather than a live OAuth integration, this generates a CSV in QuickBooks Online's own native invoice-import format (Settings → Import Data → Invoices) for every job scheduled in the range that has moved past a quote (booked or further). Line items exactly match what the same job's printed Invoice shows — itemized charges when present, otherwise the single quoted-price line — so the two can never quietly disagree. QuickBooks needs a real "Item (Product/Service)" to map each line to; this fills in a generic "Moving Services" for every row, so map it once to your actual chart-of-accounts item either during the import wizard or by editing the CSV's Item column first.
